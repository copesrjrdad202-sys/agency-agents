# Data Processing Addendum

Effective date: 2026-08-21

This DPA applies when the service provider processes personal data on behalf of a business customer.

## 1. Roles
- Customer: controller / business customer
- Service provider: processor for customer data

## 2. Processing Instructions
We process personal data only:
- to provide the service
- on documented customer instructions
- as needed for security, support, and legal compliance

## 3. Subprocessors
Subprocessors may include:
- Twilio
- Stripe
- Google
- ElevenLabs
- cloud hosting and logging providers

## 4. Security
We maintain reasonable technical and organizational measures, including access control, least privilege, and audit logging where available.

## 5. Data Subject Requests
We will reasonably assist customers with access, deletion, correction, and other applicable requests.

## 6. Breach Notice
We will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting customer data.

## 7. Return / Deletion
At termination, we will delete or return customer data as instructed, subject to lawful retention requirements.

## 8. International Transfers
If data is transferred across borders, the parties will use an appropriate transfer mechanism where required by law.
