# 🔄 Runbook: Social-to-Service Flywheel

> **Mode**: NEXUS-Sprint | **Purpose**: Make social acquisition and service delivery move as one system

---

## Goal

Turn social content into booked calls, booked jobs, invoices, payments, and reviews without manual handoff chaos.

## System Rule

One lead should move through one shared pipeline:

**Content -> Click -> Lead -> Booking -> Service -> Invoice -> Payment -> Review**

## Shared Ownership

| Stage | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Topic selection | Social Media Strategist | Campaign brief |
| Content creation | Content Creator | Draft queue |
| Publish approval | Brand Guardian + Legal Compliance Checker | Approved post |
| Publish action | Social Media Automation Agent | Scheduled/published post |
| Lead capture | Analytics Reporter | Lead record |
| Lead routing | Business Context Agent | Correct business profile |
| Booking | Calendar Agent | Appointment confirmation |
| Billing | Invoice Agent | Invoice or payment link |
| Payment | Payment Agent | Paid status |
| Follow-up | Social Media Strategist + Support Responder | Review request / nurture |

## Flow Rules

- Every post must point to one clear CTA
- Every CTA must resolve to one business ID
- Every lead must be assigned within 15 minutes
- Every booked job must generate a status update back to the campaign owner
- Every paid job should trigger a review or testimonial request

## Feedback Loop

1. Social team publishes content.
2. Analytics reports clicks and leads.
3. Business Context Agent identifies the right business.
4. Calendar Agent books the job.
5. Invoice and Payment Agents close the loop.
6. P&L Dashboard Agent measures revenue impact.
7. Social Media Strategist adjusts future content from real numbers.

## Operational Safeguards

- Use separate credentials for each platform
- Keep campaign analytics read-only
- Use approval gates before publish
- Never let social automation bypass business context
- Never let leads stay unassigned

## Daily Operating Cadence

- Morning: review yesterday's content, leads, and booked jobs
- Midday: answer comments/DMs, route questions
- Evening: reconcile campaign performance with bookings and revenue

## Success Criteria

- Lead assignment time under 15 minutes
- Booking conversion from social traffic tracked by business ID
- Revenue attributed back to the campaign
- Reviews/testimonials collected from completed jobs
- No manual copying of lead data between systems
