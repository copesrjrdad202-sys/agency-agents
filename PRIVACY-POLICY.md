# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-21

This policy explains how we process data when you use the AI receptionist and business automation services.

## 1. Data We Process
We may process:
- business contact details
- customer phone numbers and messages
- appointment details
- invoice and payment records
- tax and bookkeeping records
- call logs and support history

## 2. Purposes
We use data to:
- answer calls and messages
- book and manage appointments
- generate invoices and track payments
- maintain business records and tax summaries
- provide support and troubleshooting
- improve reliability and security

## 3. Legal Bases
Depending on the context, processing may rely on:
- contract performance
- legitimate interests
- consent
- legal obligation

## 4. Sharing
We may share data with service providers that help us operate the service, including Twilio, Stripe, Google, and ElevenLabs.

## 5. Retention
We keep data only as long as needed for the service, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and recordkeeping.

## 6. Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No system is perfectly secure.

## 7. Your Rights
Depending on jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict certain processing.

## 8. Children
The service is not intended for children.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy from time to time.
