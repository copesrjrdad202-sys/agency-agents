## TIPS FOR SENDING THESE

### Best Practices:

1. **Personalization is KEY**
   - Don't send generic templates
   - Research the company: "I noticed you've been in business X years"
   - Reference their service area or recent growth
   - Mention competitor or peer: "I'm working with 3 other plumbers in your area"

2. **Send at Right Time**
   - Plumbers: Tuesday-Thursday (off busy weekends)
   - HVAC: Off-season or early morning (before call rush)
   - Electricians: Mid-week, 10am-12pm (quieter times)

3. **Keep It Short**
   - 3-5 paragraphs max
   - One clear CTA (call to action)
   - Make scanning easy

4. **Use Social Proof**
   - As soon as you have 5+ customers, mention them
   - Use their first name + location
   - Real results = massive credibility

5. **Follow-Up Sequence**
   - Day 1: First email
   - Day 5: First follow-up ("just following up...")
   - Day 10: Last follow-up ("last one...")
   - Day 15: Stop (move to next prospect)

### Tools to Send These:
- **Apollo.io** ($50/mo) — email sequences + lead lists
- **Sales Loft** ($80/mo) — enterprise email tool
- **Mailchimp** (free tier) — simple sequences
- **Outreach** ($60/mo) — best for tracking

### Expected Results:
- **Send rate:** 100 emails/week
- **Open rate:** 25-35% (good subject lines)
- **Reply rate:** 3-5% (15-50 replies)
- **Demo rate:** 30% of replies (5-15 demos/week)
- **Close rate:** 30-50% of demos (1-7 customers/week)

## A/B TESTING IDEAS

**Test different subject lines:**
1. "How many calls did you miss today?" ← Direct pain
2. "Your competitors are already using this" ← Urgency
3. "Calendly vs. AI Receptionist (big difference)" ← Comparison
4. "One change that paid for itself in 5 days" ← ROI focus
5. "Quick question about your booking process" ← Curiosity

**Track:**
- Which subject line gets highest open rate?
- Which email body gets most replies?
- Which CTA gets most demo bookings?
- Optimize based on data

Send 20 of each, track results, scale the winners.

---

## DO NOT DO THIS

❌ Don't pitch in first email (build interest first)
❌ Don't send to same person twice in one week
❌ Don't make it too long (keep to 3-4 paragraphs)
❌ Don't be pushy or salesy ("Act now!" "Limited time!")
❌ Don't forget to follow up (70% close on follow-up, not first email)
❌ Don't send at 2am (send 9am-12pm their time zone)
