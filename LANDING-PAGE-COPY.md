## DESIGN NOTES

### Color Scheme:
- **Primary (CTA buttons):** Bright blue (#0066CC)
- **Secondary (Highlights):** Orange (#FF6600) for urgency
- **Neutral (Background):** Light gray (#F5F5F5)
- **Text:** Dark gray (#333333)

### Typography:
- **Headlines:** Bold, large, clear
- **Body copy:** Readable, 16px minimum
- **Call-to-action:** Bright, contrasting color, large button

### Layout:
- Mobile-first (works great on phones)
- Sections stacked vertically
- Maximum width: 1200px (desktop)
- White space between sections

### Visual Elements:
- Mockup of AI answering call + calendar + invoice
- Comparison chart (before/after)
- Customer photos + testimonials
- Trust badges (industry certifications)
- Social proof numbers (big, bold)

### Conversions:
- Headline + subheadline hook users immediately
- Problem section gets attention
- Solution section shows the answer
- ROI section closes the sale
- CTA buttons appear 3+ times on page
- Email capture for demo booking
