# Legal Launch Checklist

## Before go-live
- [ ] Primary reviewer: Legal Compliance Checker
- [ ] Privacy reviewer: Data Privacy Officer
- [ ] Review Terms of Service with counsel
- [ ] Review Privacy Policy with counsel
- [ ] Review Data Processing Addendum with counsel
- [ ] Confirm subprocessors in the policy match production vendors
- [ ] Confirm retention periods for invoices, calls, and logs
- [ ] Confirm deletion process for customer data
- [ ] Confirm consent language for call recording / SMS if needed
- [ ] Confirm website footer links to legal pages

## During onboarding
- [ ] Show customer the legal docs
- [ ] Capture acceptance of Terms
- [ ] Capture DPA acceptance where needed
- [ ] Confirm customer is authorized to process their customer data

## Operational controls
- [ ] Keep secrets out of source control
- [ ] Log admin actions
- [ ] Rotate keys on offboarding
- [ ] Review subprocessors quarterly
