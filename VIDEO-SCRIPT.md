## SCRIPT NOTES FOR RECORDING

### Pacing:
- Speak clearly, not too fast (people watch at 1.5x or 2x speed)
- Pause between sections (gives viewers time to absorb)
- Emphasize numbers and ROI (slow down, let it sink in)

### Tone:
- Confident but not aggressive
- Understanding (acknowledge the pain)
- Solution-focused (here's the fix)
- Friendly (you're not alone)

### Visual Timing:
- Change visuals every 3-5 seconds (keeps attention)
- Text overlays should match voiceover
- Use smooth transitions between sections
- Show real dashboard/interface (not mockups if possible)

### Recording Tips:
1. **Audio Quality:** Use a good microphone. Bad audio kills views.
2. **Background:** Quiet room, no echoes.
3. **Script:** Use teleprompter or have it on a monitor to your side.
4. **Multiple Takes:** Record each section 2-3 times, use the best one.
5. **Energy:** Even if you're tired, bring energy to your voice.

### Screen Recording (Dashboard Demo):
- Record at 1920x1080 (full HD)
- Move mouse slowly (not too fast)
- Click deliberately (so viewers know where to look)
- Pause on key numbers (let them sink in)

### Editing:
- Music: Background instrumental (YouTube royalty-free library)
- Transitions: Simple cuts (not fancy effects)
- Title cards: Show at key moments (Problem, Solution, ROI, etc.)
- Color correction: Make sure dashboard/UI looks crisp
- Subtitles: Add captions (YouTube auto-generates, clean them up)
